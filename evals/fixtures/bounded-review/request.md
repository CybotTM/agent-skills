# Debit review

Read-only adversarial review of check.py and its caller in service.py.
A debit must accept an amount greater than zero and no greater than the balance.
Only the owner may debit an account. The supplied files are the review artifacts;
identify them by content hashes when recording evidence.

Available local read-only inspection and review are authorized. An additional
external model, disclosure or paid CLI invocation has not been authorized.
Do not edit the sources, merge or deploy. If a fresh-context reviewer cannot run,
disclose that limit instead of claiming it ran.
